# Discovery brief

Use this compact structure:

```markdown
## Problem
What user or product problem is being solved?

## Outcome
What observable behavior proves the problem is addressed?

## Evidence
What existing screens, routes, state, APIs, tests, or reports constrain the solution?

## Assumptions and decisions
Which facts are verified, which are assumptions, and which decisions are still open?

## Scope
What is the smallest slice that can deliver the outcome?

## Non-goals
What will explicitly not change?

## Acceptance criteria
What can be tested or observed?

## Risks and next mode
What could invalidate the plan, and should the next step be Analyze, Plan, Change, Review, or Release?
```

Prefer an existing ALIVE boundary over a new one. A new dependency or cross-layer change requires an explicit reason, comparison, and verification plan.
